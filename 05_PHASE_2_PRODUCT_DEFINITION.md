## 3. MVP scope principles

Include only what is required to complete the selected specialty's
primary workflow reliably: - identity and secure access; - clinic
workspace and practitioner profile; - patient records and search; -
appointments and daily schedule; - clinical encounter documentation; -
specialty-specific templates and objects; - prescriptions or documents
only where validated; - billing/collections only to the minimum
validated level; - essential reminders; - audit trail, data export, and
safe account lifecycle.

The exact clinical module must be defined after specialty selection. Do
not assume a dental chart, dermatology photo timeline, or physiotherapy
plan before choosing the specialty.

## 4. Explicitly deferred unless evidence changes

-   multi-specialty product breadth;
-   hospital/enterprise workflows;
-   complex inventory and accounting;
-   patient super-app;
-   autonomous diagnosis or treatment recommendations;
-   advanced AI features;
-   extensive third-party integrations;
-   multi-branch administration;
-   offline-first synchronization if not essential to the validated
    workflow.

## 5. Prototype test criteria

Test at least: - create/find a patient; - schedule or change an
appointment; - start and complete a visit; - use the specialty-specific
documentation flow; - save and retrieve a record; - understand next
steps and follow-up; - recover from a validation error; - complete
common tasks one-handed where practical.

Record task completion, time, errors, requests for help, and user
confidence.

## 6. Exit gate G2

Pass when: - one specialty is selected with documented evidence; -
prototype testing identifies no unresolved fatal usability issue; - MVP
scope is bounded and prioritized; - every P0 requirement has acceptance
criteria; - pilot proposition has credible interest; - legal and
security requirements are represented in the PRD.

## 7. Deliverables

-   Specialty decision record.
-   Product Requirements Document.
-   User journeys and story map.
-   Prototype and usability findings.
-   MVP feature matrix.
-   Non-functional requirements.
-   Pilot proposition.
-   Updated financial and roadmap assumptions.
