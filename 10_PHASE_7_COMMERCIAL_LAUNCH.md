# 10 --- Phase 7: Commercial Launch

**Indicative duration:** 3--4 weeks after successful Pilot gate.

## 1. Launch objectives

-   Make the product commercially available to a controlled initial
    segment.
-   Ensure onboarding, support, billing, legal terms, and service
    operations are ready.
-   Acquire customers through measurable channels.
-   Avoid scaling acquisition before retention and unit economics are
    understood.

## 2. Launch readiness checklist

### Product

-   P0 and launch-critical P1 workflows pass.
-   Known limitations are documented.
-   Mobile performance and usability are acceptable.
-   Data export and account lifecycle are tested.
-   Product analytics exclude unnecessary sensitive data.

### Security and privacy

-   No open critical security findings.
-   Legal review and required customer-facing documents are complete.
-   Access control, audit, backups, restore, monitoring, and incident
    procedures are active.
-   Vendor and subprocessor responsibilities are documented.

### Commercial

-   Pricing and plan limits are approved.
-   Terms, privacy notice, cancellation, refund, and data export rules
    are clear.
-   Payment flow and subscription state are tested.
-   No unapproved price or savings claim is published.

### Support and operations

-   Onboarding guide and knowledge base are ready.
-   Support channel and escalation ownership are defined.
-   Incident severity and response process are rehearsed.
-   Service status and customer communication process are defined.

### Marketing and sales

-   Positioning is supported by research.
-   Landing page claims are accurate and demonstrable.
-   Case studies/testimonials have permission and are truthful.
-   Lead tracking and conversion definitions are configured.
-   Acquisition budget is explicitly approved.

## 3. Launch approach

Prefer a controlled release: 1. Existing pilot clinics. 2. Referrals and
direct outreach to the validated specialty. 3. Small campaign with a
capped budget. 4. Review activation, retention, support, and conversion.
5. Expand only when operational capacity is adequate.

## 4. Commercial metrics

-   qualified leads;
-   demo or trial conversion;
-   activation rate;
-   time to first value;
-   trial-to-paid conversion;
-   monthly recurring revenue (MRR);
-   churn and retention;
-   customer acquisition cost (CAC);
-   gross margin;
-   support cost per clinic;
-   net revenue retention if applicable.

## 5. Exit gate G7

Pass when product, legal, security, payment, support, and marketing
readiness are approved and the business can onboard and support
customers without relying on undocumented heroics.
