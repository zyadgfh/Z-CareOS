# 11 --- Phase 8: Stabilization & Growth

**Initial review window:** first 8--12 weeks after launch; ongoing
thereafter.

## 1. Stabilization priorities

-   Fix reliability and performance issues.
-   Reduce onboarding friction.
-   Improve the highest-frequency workflows.
-   Reduce support tickets and repeated user confusion.
-   Improve data import/export reliability.
-   Review security alerts, access logs, and incident trends.
-   Validate backup and recovery on a recurring schedule.

## 2. Growth experiments

Every experiment must include a hypothesis, audience, success metric,
guardrail, time window, and stop condition.

Potential experiments: - specialty-specific onboarding; - referral
program; - educational content and demonstrations; - partnerships with
relevant professional communities; - improved trial activation; -
pricing/packaging tests; - workflow templates based on observed needs.

Do not run experiments that imply clinical efficacy or medical outcomes
without appropriate evidence and review.

## 3. Expansion criteria

A second specialty, web surface, patient portal, multi-branch
capability, offline mode, or new geography should be considered only
when: - the current segment shows sustained retention; - the primary
workflow is stable; - support and operating costs are understood; -
current customers demonstrate demand; - the expansion does not
compromise tenant isolation or privacy; - legal and regulatory
implications are reviewed; - the business case is positive.

## 4. Expansion decision scorecard

Score each proposed expansion: - customer evidence and urgency ---
25%; - retention/revenue impact --- 20%; - strategic fit --- 15%; -
implementation effort --- 15%; - security/legal complexity --- 15%; -
support and operating burden --- 10%.

Document uncertainty and opportunity cost.

## 5. Exit gate G8

Approve expansion only when repeatable acquisition and retention are
visible, the product is operationally stable, unit economics are
credible, and the proposed expansion has evidence-backed demand.
