## Required baseline

-   Product: CareOS.
-   Customer: solo practitioner.
-   Platform: mobile-first.
-   Pilot: hybrid.
-   Architecture: hybrid modular.
-   Specialty: research-selected; not yet approved.
-   Development/test data: synthetic.
-   Initial geography: Egypt.
-   Current strategic status: Continue Validation.

## Exit gate G0

Pass when the strategy baseline, open decisions, assumptions, owners,
and research protocol are documented and approved.

## Out of scope

-   Coding.
-   Database creation or migration.
-   Cloud provisioning.
-   Production configuration.
-   Real patient-data collection.
-   Final specialty selection without evidence.
